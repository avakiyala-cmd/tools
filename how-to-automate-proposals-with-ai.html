The Problem with Manual Proposals

Every proposal has the same sections: executive summary, scope of work, deliverables, timeline, pricing, terms. You're not reinventing the wheel each time - you're rewriting the same wheel with different specifications.

That's exactly what AI is good at: taking a pattern and filling it with new specifics.

How AI Proposal Generation Works

You provide a brief: client name, project type, scope, budget range, timeline. AI generates a complete proposal with:

- Executive summary tailored to the client's industry
- Scope of work with specific deliverables
- Timeline with milestones
- Pricing table
- Terms and conditions

The output isn't a generic template. It's a contextual first draft that understands the difference between a website redesign proposal and a brand strategy proposal.
